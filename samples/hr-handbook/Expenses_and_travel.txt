Northwind Labs Employee Handbook (SAMPLE): Expenses and travel

This is a sample document for trying the Knowledge assistant. Northwind Labs is a made-up company.

What you can claim
Reasonable costs of doing your job: travel to client sites and other offices, hotels on business trips, meals while travelling, and equipment your manager approved in advance. Commuting to your usual office isn't an expense.

Limits
Hotels: up to 180 USD a night in major cities, 120 USD elsewhere, before tax.
Meals while travelling: up to 60 USD a day, receipts required.
Flights: economy class for trips under 6 hours; premium economy for longer ones.
Trains: standard class; first class only if it's cheaper.
Taxis and ride shares: when public transport isn't practical, or after 21:00.

How to claim
Submit claims in the Expenses app within 30 days of spending the money, with a photo of every receipt over 10 USD. Your manager approves them; Finance pays approved claims in the next monthly payroll. Claims older than 90 days aren't paid.

Company card
Employees who travel more than twice a month can ask Finance for a company card. Card spending follows the same limits and still needs receipts in the Expenses app within 30 days.

Booking travel
Book flights and hotels through the travel portal, at least 14 days ahead when possible. Trips abroad need your manager's approval before booking and a check with People Ops on visas.

Home office
Everyone gets a one-off home office allowance of 500 USD for a desk, chair, screen or similar, claimed like any other expense within the first 6 months.
