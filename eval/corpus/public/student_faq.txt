Cloudastra Academy — Student FAQ

How do I log in to my classes?
All classes, recordings and assignments are on the Astra LMS. You receive your login by email
within 24 hours of enrolling. Reset your password from the LMS sign-in page.

What attendance is required?
You must attend at least 75% of live classes to receive a certificate. If you fall below 75%,
you can join the next batch once at no extra cost to make up the missed classes.

Are classes recorded?
Yes. Every live class is recorded and posted on the LMS within 12 hours. Recordings stay
available for 90 days after your course ends.

Can I switch to a different batch?
You can move to another batch of the same course once, if you ask before the halfway point
of your current batch. Write to the student success team from the LMS help page.

What if I miss an assignment deadline?
Assignments are accepted up to 3 days late with a 10% penalty. After that they score zero,
unless the student success team approved an extension for medical reasons.

Do you offer EMI or instalments?
Courses above 5000 rupees can be paid in 3 monthly instalments at no extra cost.

Is there a placement guarantee?
No. We do not guarantee jobs. The careers team runs mock interviews and shares openings from
our hiring partners with students who complete DS-300 or PY-110.
