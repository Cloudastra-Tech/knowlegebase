Cloudastra Academy — Student Data and Privacy Policy

What we collect:
We collect a student's name, email address, phone number, payment records, attendance and
assignment grades. We do not collect Aadhaar numbers or any government ID beyond what an
invoice legally requires.

How long we keep it:
Student records (enrolment, grades and certificates) are kept for 7 years after the course
ends, so certificates can still be verified. Payment records are kept for 8 years, as Indian
tax law requires. Class recordings are deleted 90 days after the course ends.

Who can see it:
Teachers see the names, attendance and grades of students in their own batches only.
Payment data is visible to the finance team only. We never sell student data, and we do not
share it with hiring partners without the student's written consent.

Your rights:
Students can download a copy of their data from the Astra LMS profile page at any time.
To ask for corrections or deletion, write to the data protection officer through the LMS help
page; requests are answered within 30 days. Records we must keep by law are deleted when the
legal retention period ends.

Breaches:
If student data is exposed, affected students are told within 72 hours of the academy
learning about it.
