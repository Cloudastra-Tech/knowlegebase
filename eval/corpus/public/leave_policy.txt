Cloudastra Academy — Leave and Holiday Policy (HR-02)

Who this covers:
This policy applies to every full-time employee of Cloudastra Academy, including teachers,
program coordinators and operations staff. Interns and contractors follow the terms in their
own agreements.

Annual leave:
Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave is credited at 1.5 days per month of service.
Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward into the next year; anything above 10 days lapses
on 31 December. Unused leave is not paid out in cash.

Sick leave:
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's certificate is required for
more than 3 consecutive sick days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Parental leave:
Birthing parents get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Other parents get 4 weeks of paid
parental leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

Work from home:
Employees may work from home up to 2 days a week with their manager's approval.
Teachers must be on campus on the days they teach in-person batches.

Public holidays:
The academy is closed on 10 public holidays a year. The list is published every December
on the HR notice board. Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti and Diwali are always
included.

How to apply:
Apply for leave in the Astra HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick
leave. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days.
