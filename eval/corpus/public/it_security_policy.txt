Cloudastra Academy — IT Security Policy (SEC-04)

Accounts and passwords:
Every staff account must use a password of at least 14 characters. Passwords are never
shared, written down or reused across work and personal accounts.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the Astra LMS admin panel and the
Astra HR portal. Use the authenticator app; SMS codes are not allowed.

Laptops and devices:
Work laptops are issued with full-disk encryption turned on. Do not disable it.
The screen must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
A lost or stolen laptop must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours, so the device can
be wiped remotely.
Personal phones may access work email only through the approved mail app with a device PIN.

Network access:
Use the company VPN (WireGuard) on any network outside the campus, including home Wi-Fi.
Never connect a work laptop to public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

Phishing:
Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in the mail app. Do not forward
them. The security team reviews every report within one working day.
A phishing simulation is run every quarter; staff who click the simulated link repeat the
security awareness training.

Software:
Only install software from the approved software catalogue. Requests for anything else go to
the IT helpdesk and need security approval.

Incidents:
Any suspected security incident (a leaked password, malware, data sent to the wrong person)
must be reported to the IT helpdesk immediately. Do not try to investigate it yourself.
