# New staff onboarding guide

Welcome to Cloudastra Academy. This guide walks you through your first two weeks: what
happens on day one, the accounts you will get, the equipment you can borrow and who to ask
when something is unclear. Keep it handy until your buddy signs off your onboarding checklist.

## Day one

Arrive at 9:30am. Your manager meets you at reception and introduces your onboarding buddy,
a colleague from another team who answers the questions you would rather not ask your
manager. The morning is a walk around the office, the fire exits and the first-aid kits.
After lunch you collect your laptop from the IT desk on the second floor and sign the
equipment register. The day ends with a 30-minute welcome call with the founders.

## Accounts and access

### Email and calendar

Your email address is created before you join and its first password is sent to your
personal email. Change it on your first login. Calendar invitations for the first week are
already in your calendar.

### Second floor printer

The shared printer on the second floor is not added automatically. On a Linux or Mac laptop,
run this once from a terminal, then pick "cloudastra-2f" in any print dialog:

```bash
lpadmin -p cloudastra-2f -E -v ipp://print.cloudastra.local/2f -m everywhere
lpoptions -d cloudastra-2f
```

Windows laptops get the printer from the IT desk on day one.

### Learning platform

Teaching staff get an instructor account on the learning platform within three working days.
Ask the academic coordinator if it has not arrived by then.

## Equipment you can borrow

Headsets, webcams and portable monitors can be borrowed from the IT desk for up to 30 days.
Write the item and the return date in the equipment register. A second monitor for home
working can be borrowed for the whole of your probation period once your manager approves it.

## Expense claims

Expense claims are submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with a
photo of the receipt. Travel booked by the company is not claimed again. Meals during a work
trip are covered up to 800 rupees a day. Claims are reviewed every Friday and paid with the
next salary. A claim older than 30 days needs a note from your manager explaining the delay.
Fuel for your own car is claimed per kilometre at the rate the finance team publishes each
quarter; tolls are claimed at cost with their receipts.
