Cloudastra Academy — Hiring and Restructuring Plan 2027 (CONFIDENTIAL — leadership and HR only)

Summary:
The academy will open a new campus in Pune in April 2027 and close the Hyderabad centre on
31 March 2027. Hyderabad batches move online; no learner loses a seat.

Hiring:
We will hire 6 teachers and 2 program coordinators for the Pune campus between January and
March 2027. The hiring budget for 2027 is 1.2 crore rupees, including recruiter fees.

Hyderabad staff:
The 9 employees at the Hyderabad centre will be offered a transfer to Pune or to the online
team first. Anyone who declines receives 3 months of severance. This must not be discussed
with staff before the all-hands on 15 January 2027.

Risks:
If the Pune lease is delayed, the opening moves to June 2027 and Hyderabad stays open until
then. The board reviews this plan again in December 2026.
